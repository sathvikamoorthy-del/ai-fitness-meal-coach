# Intent

## Purpose
Let an individual set a fitness goal, get AI workout and meal plans, log workouts, meals, weight and water, and see their progress over time.

## Goals
- G1: A new user can set a profile and goal and get an AI plan.
- G2: Logging workouts and meals takes less than 30 seconds.
- G3: Daily calories and weekly progress are always correct.
- G4: The app can be deployed (Express, Turso).

## Not goals
- No payments or subscriptions.
- No wearable or device integration.
- No social feed or sharing between users; one user per account, no gyms, trainers or organisations.
- No mobile app.
- No medical advice.

## Success
- A new user signs in, sets a profile and goal, and receives an AI workout plan and meal plan.
- Logging a workout or a meal takes less than 30 seconds.
- Daily calories and weekly progress always match what the user logged.
- The app runs deployed on Express with Turso.
