# User can swap a meal with AI

Requirement REQ-414 · Feature FEAT-1377 · Decision DEC-449

## Purpose
As a user, I can ask the AI to replace a meal I do not like.

Keeps the meal plan usable when a meal does not suit (brief feature 14).

## Behaviour
The user picks a meal in their plan and asks the AI to replace it.

## Acceptance criteria
- The user can ask to replace one meal in their plan.
- The replacement takes the place of that meal only.
- The replacement respects the user's dietary preference and allergies.

## Order
Order: 11 of 24
Depends on: User gets an AI meal plan
Goal served: brief only
