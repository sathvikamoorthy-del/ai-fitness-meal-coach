# User can log water intake

Requirement REQ-420 · Feature FEAT-1383 · Decision DEC-455

## Purpose
As a user, I can record the water I drink each day.

Water logging is part of the purpose of the app.

## Behaviour
The user records water drunk each day and sees the day's total.

## Acceptance criteria
- The user can record an amount of water for a day.
- The day's total equals the sum of the amounts recorded that day.
